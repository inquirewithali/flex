The new standard for hiring.

flex was built on one idea: the résumé is a broken interface for an even more broken hiring process. We're building what comes next.

Why we exist

Hiring today rewards prestige and pedigree more than capability. The single biggest predictor of whether someone gets an interview is the school name and brand-name employer at the top of their résumé — not whether they can do the job.

That filter excludes most of the workforce. Self-taught builders. Trade school graduates. People who took non-linear paths. Anyone who can't list a FAANG-tier brand or an Ivy on page one.

flex is what hiring should be: a verified, evidence-based view of your skills, reliability, and real results — without your school or photo getting in the way until you say so.
