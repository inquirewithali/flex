      Common questions

      What counts as a "successful hire"?

      A successful hire is when a candidate sourced through flex accepts an offer and starts work. You're not charged the per-hire fee until the candidate's start date. If they don't start, no charge.

      How does the per-hire fee compare to agencies?

      Traditional staffing agencies charge 15–25% of first-year salary, which works out to roughly $11,000–$18,000 for a $75,000 role. flex's $300 per-hire fee on the Growth plan is a 96% reduction — for the same outcome, with verified candidates.

      Is there a free trial?

      Yes. Starter and Growth come with a 14-day free trial — full access, no credit card required to start. Cancel anytime during the trial with no charge.

      What ATS systems do you integrate with?

      Growth and Enterprise plans include native integrations with Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, and SmartRecruiters. Custom integrations available on Enterprise.

      Do annual plans really save 20%?

      Yes — switching to annual billing reduces the monthly fee by 20%. Per-hire fees are unchanged. Most teams that hire 25+ per year see immediate ROI within their first quarter.

      What happens to my data if I cancel?

      You keep access to all your data for 30 days after cancellation. We provide a full export in CSV/JSON format. After 30 days, your data is permanently deleted (in compliance with GDPR & CCPA).

      Are bias audits really included?

      Yes — bias audits are a flex commitment, not an upsell. Starter gets an annual audit, Growth gets quarterly (4× the legal requirement under NYC LL144), and Enterprise gets continuous monitoring with monthly reporting.

      Can I migrate from another platform?

      Enterprise plans include white-glove migration from Greenhouse, Workday, Eightfold, and similar systems. Migration typically takes 2–3 weeks with zero downtime.
